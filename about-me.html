My story

Turning real-world problems into simple tools

I matriculated from Hoërskool Kanonkop in Middelburg, Mpumalanga in 2023, then moved to Benoni to study at Boston City Campus, where I completed a Higher Certificate in Mobile Application Development.

Studying mobile development gave me a strong foundation in how software is planned, designed, built and tested. Since then I’ve kept growing across the stack: JavaScript for apps and the web, Java for robust back-ends, Python for automation and data, and WordPress for client-friendly websites.

I like work that people actually use: clear interfaces, tidy code and honest communication about what’s possible. I’m currently open to junior developer roles, internships and freelance projects.
